# Privacy Policy for Twitch Bonus Claimer

**Last Updated:** December 27, 2025

The **Twitch Bonus Claimer** extension ("the Extension") is designed to prioritize user privacy. This policy outlines how data is handled.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data.**

-   **No Analytics:** The Extension does not use Google Analytics or any third-party tracking software.
-   **No External Servers:** The Extension operates entirely client-side within your browser. No data is sent to external servers.
-   **Local Storage:** The Extension uses your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely to:
    -   Save your preference (Enabled/Disabled).
    -   Store a temporary log of your recent claims (which stays on your device).

## Permissions

The Extension requests the minimum permissions required to function:
-   **Storage:** To save your settings and local logs.
-   **Host Permission (`https://*.twitch.tv/*`)**: To allow the content script to detect and click the bonus button on Twitch pages.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please create an issue on our GitHub repository.
