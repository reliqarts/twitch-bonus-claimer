## Summary (Max 132 chars)
Automatically claim Twitch Channel Points (Bonus Chests) while you watch. Lightweight, privacy-focused, and undetectable.

## Detailed Description

**Never miss a Bonus Chest again!** 🎁

Twitch Bonus Claimer is a simple, set-it-and-forget-it extension that automatically collects Channel Points for you on any open Twitch stream. Whether you're actively watching, lurking in a tab, or grabbing a snack, your points will keep growing.

**🚀 Key Features:**

*   **Smart Auto-Claim**: Instantly detects when the "Bonus Chest" button appears and clicks it for you.
*   **Human-Like Behavior**: Uses randomized delays (0.5s - 2s) to simulate natural user clicks, keeping your account safe.
*   **Multi-Tab Support**: Works seamlessly across multiple open streams simultaneously.
*   **Privacy First**: Runs 100% on your device. No data is collected, stored, or sent to external servers.
*   **Clean Dashboard**: A beautiful, dark-mode friendly popup shows you exactly how many times you've claimed a bonus and lets you toggle the extension on or off in a single click.

**🔒 Privacy & Security:**
We believe in transparency. This extension requires minimal permissions ("storage") to save your settings and operates securely within your browser. 

**Open Source:**
This project is open source! You can view the code on GitHub to verify safety and security.
